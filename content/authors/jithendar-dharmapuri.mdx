Jithendar Dharmapuri is a Lead AI Platform Engineer at S&P Global in Singapore and the editor of this publication. He has worked in infrastructure and platform engineering since 2017, at S&P Global, Adecco, EPAM Systems, HCL Technologies and Wipro among others.

At S&P Global he built an AI-assisted incident knowledge application that answers natural-language questions about past incidents, set up observability with Prometheus and Grafana, and automated infrastructure provisioning with Terraform and GitHub Actions.

He writes about AI engineering, AWS, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code and observability, from systems he has built and run. He is an **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional** and works mainly in Python, Go and Terraform.
