We use AI tools to help with our work, and we are open about how.

## Where AI may be used

- **Discovery**: monitoring official feeds (for example cloud provider, Kubernetes and security advisory feeds) and grouping related items into topics for an editor to consider.
- **Drafting**: producing a first draft from source material an editor has selected and approved.
- **Editing help**: suggesting headlines, summaries and links to related articles.

## What AI never does here

- AI never publishes, approves or merges content. Every article is reviewed and approved by a human editor first.
- AI drafts may only contain facts that can be traced to the supplied sources. Uncertain claims are flagged for the editor to verify or remove.
- AI is not used to invent quotes, benchmarks, test results or experiences.

## Responsibility

The editor is responsible for everything published, however it was drafted. Errors are handled under our [corrections policy](/corrections-policy).
