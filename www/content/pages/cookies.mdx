## Necessary

These are needed for the site to work and are not used for tracking:

- **Sign-in session** (only if you sign in): keeps you signed in. Set by this site.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile** (on sign-in, comment and contact forms): checks that a person is submitting the form.

## Analytics (only if enabled)

Google Analytics 4 measures which pages are read. Its cookies (names starting with `_ga`) are set only when analytics storage is allowed. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland that requires your consent.

## Advertising (only if enabled)

Google AdSense may set cookies to show and measure ads, and to remember your consent choice. Personalised ads in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland require your consent, collected by Google's certified consent message (IAB TCF).

## Changing your choice

Use **Privacy choices** in the footer to review or withdraw consent. The link appears where Google's consent message applies. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
