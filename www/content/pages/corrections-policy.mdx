We correct errors promptly and openly.

## Reporting an error

Send the article link and a description of the problem, ideally with a source, through our [contact page](/contact).

## How corrections appear

- A **Corrections** section is added to the end of the article, with the date and a note describing what changed.
- The article's "Updated" time is set to the time of the correction.
- We do not silently change facts. Typos and formatting fixes that do not change meaning may be made without a note.

## Removals

We remove articles only in exceptional cases, for example for legal reasons or where an article cannot be corrected. When that happens, the reason is noted where possible.
