**Jithendar.com is written by an engineer who ships.** Every tutorial, analysis and architecture write-up here comes from systems that were actually designed, built, broken and fixed, not from press releases.

It is two things at once: a specialist publication on AI engineering, cloud and Kubernetes, and the engineering portfolio of [Jithendar Dharmapuri](/authors/jithendar-dharmapuri).

## Hi, I'm Jithendar

Today I spend most of my time where cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes and AI meet: turning ambitious architecture diagrams into platforms that teams can operate with confidence.

## What you will find here

- **News**: timely, sourced reports on releases, advisories and announcements.
- **Analysis**: what a development means and who it affects.
- **Tutorials**: step-by-step guides you can follow on your own infrastructure.
- **Opinion**: clearly labelled personal views.
- **Curated summaries**: short digests of one or more sources, always linked.
- **Projects**: engineering work, with the architecture behind it.

Topics span AI engineering, AI agents, the Model Context Protocol, LLMOps and MLOps; AWS and cloud architecture; Kubernetes and the cloud-native ecosystem; DevOps, SRE and platform engineering; infrastructure as code; and security for cloud and Kubernetes workloads. Every article carries a visible label for its type, so you always know what you are reading.

:::quote
Every article is reviewed and approved by a human editor before it is published.
:::

## How articles are made

News and curated summaries must cite their sources, and those sources are listed on the page. Read the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy), the [AI use policy](/ai-use-policy) and the [corrections policy](/corrections-policy) for the details.

## How the site is built

The site is a Next.js application running on a self-managed Kubernetes cluster. GitHub Actions lints, tests and builds every change, then packages it as a container image in Amazon ECR before it is rolled out. The site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising scripts; see the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
