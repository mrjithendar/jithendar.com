Jithendar.com is a specialist technology publication and the engineering portfolio of [Jithendar Dharmapuri](/authors/jithendar-dharmapuri).

## What we cover

- AI engineering, AI agents, the Model Context Protocol, LLMOps and MLOps
- AWS and cloud architecture
- Kubernetes and the cloud-native ecosystem
- DevOps, site reliability engineering and platform engineering
- Infrastructure as code
- Cybersecurity for cloud and Kubernetes workloads

## What you will find here

- **News**: timely, sourced reports on releases, advisories and announcements.
- **Analysis**: what a development means and who it affects.
- **Tutorials**: step-by-step guides.
- **Opinion**: clearly labelled personal views.
- **Curated summaries**: short digests of one or more sources, always linked.
- **Projects**: engineering work, with the architecture behind it.

Every article carries a visible label for its type, so you always know what you are reading.

## How articles are made

Every article is reviewed and approved by a human editor before it is published. News and curated summaries must cite their sources, and those sources are listed on the page. Read the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy), the [AI use policy](/ai-use-policy) and the [corrections policy](/corrections-policy) for the details.

## How the site is built

The site is a Next.js application. Articles are stored as files in a Git repository, so every change is reviewed and versioned. It runs on a self-managed Kubernetes cluster and is deployed with Argo CD. The site uses no cookies, analytics or advertising scripts; see the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
