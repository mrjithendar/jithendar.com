## Reading the site

When you request a page, our servers record standard request information in logs: IP address, browser user agent, the page requested, the time and a request identifier. We use it to operate, secure and debug the site.

Images and other media are stored with Cloudflare R2 and are normally delivered through this site. If a large file (such as a video) is delivered from our media domain, Cloudflare processes that request.

## Accounts, comments and contact messages

- **Accounts.** If you create an account we store your name, email address and a one-way hash of your password, plus a session record while you are signed in.
- **Comments** are stored with your account and shown publicly once a moderator approves them.
- **Contact form** messages (name, email, subject, message) are stored so the editor can read and answer them.

Sign-in, comment and contact forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person, not a bot, is submitting the form. Cloudflare processes technical information from your browser to do this.

## Analytics (only if enabled)

If Google Analytics 4 is enabled, the site sends page views (the page address without any search query) and whether an article was read to the end. We do not send names, email addresses, form contents or account identifiers. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics cookies are only used after you consent; until then Google may receive cookieless measurements, as described in Google's documentation on consent mode.

## Advertising (only if enabled)

If advertising is enabled, Google AdSense shows ads on article pages. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads. Visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's certified consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy choices** link in the footer (shown where the consent message applies). See [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Embedded videos

Some articles may embed YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode) or Vimeo videos. The video provider receives your request when the player loads.
