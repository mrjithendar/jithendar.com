## Accuracy and sourcing

- News reports and curated summaries cite at least one source, and every source is listed at the end of the article.
- We prefer primary sources: official documentation, release notes, advisories and announcements from the organisations involved.
- We do not invent quotes, benchmarks, test results or dates. When we describe hands-on testing, it was done.
- Claims we cannot verify are either removed or clearly marked as unconfirmed.

## Human review

Every article is reviewed and approved by a human editor before publication. Drafts, including any drafted with AI assistance, are submitted as changes that a person must review and accept. See the [AI use policy](/ai-use-policy).

## Content labels

Each article shows its type: **News**, **Analysis**, **Tutorial**, **Opinion**, **Curated summary** or **Project**. Opinion pieces reflect the author's view; news and analysis aim to inform.

## Dates and updates

Articles show their original publication time. An "Updated" time appears only after a substantive change to the content, never for cosmetic edits. Errors are handled under the [corrections policy](/corrections-policy).

## Independence and sponsored content

Sponsored content is labelled **Sponsored** and names the sponsor. Affiliate links, if any are ever used, will be disclosed next to the link.
