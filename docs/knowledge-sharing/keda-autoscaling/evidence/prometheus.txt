## CPU cost of a request

Least squares over the 9238 one-minute steps with no pod started in the previous 15 minutes:
  sum(rate(CPU[2m]))  ≈  0.013 cores  +  0.053 core-seconds × sum(rate(istio_requests_total{destination_workload="jithendar-com"}[2m]))
  (all callers of the pods, both Services; 0.013 cores is two idle pods)
Cross-check, the 67 minutes with more than 1.5 req/s: 0.15 cores on average for 2.36 req/s.

Derived (per pod, idle 0.0065 cores):
  5 req/s per pod   ≈ 0.0065 + 5 × 0.053  ≈ 0.27 cores  ≈ 108 % of the 250m request
  200 % of request  = 0.5 cores           ≈ (0.5 − 0.0065) / 0.053 ≈ 9 req/s per pod
